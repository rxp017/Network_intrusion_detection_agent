What do these terms mean?
Connection record
A summary of one network conversation, including its measured features.
Review recommended
The review policy queued a record for a person. It did not block traffic.
Anomaly candidate
A record classified as Normal but unusually different from the normal benchmark examples.
Model score
How strongly the model favored a class. It is not a guarantee that the class is correct.
